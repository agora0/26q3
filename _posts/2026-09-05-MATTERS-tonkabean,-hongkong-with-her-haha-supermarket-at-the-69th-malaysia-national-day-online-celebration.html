Tonkabean, HongKong with her Haha Supermarket at the 69th Malaysia National Day Online Celebration

Tonkabean from HongKong
with her Haha Supermarket at the 69th Malaysia National Day Online Celebration, jointly organized by MLYS (Malaysia Laughter Yoga Society) and JBHJC (Johor Bahru Happy and Joyous Club). It was a 45-minute celebration connecting presenters and participants from Malaysia, Singapore, Australia, Spain, Hong Kong, Germany, Cyprus, India, Austria, USA, Japan and Vietnam. A total of 49 participants was recorded at this zoom festive celebration.
